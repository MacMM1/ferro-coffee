The roastery

One roaster. One drum. No outsourcing.

Every bag sold at the counter was roasted in the room behind it, on a 12kg drum roaster run by one person. When we sell out of a roast, we sell out — we don't bulk-order in a substitute to fill the shelf.

The pour

Same ratio, same temperature, every cup.

Dialled in every morning before opening, checked again at midday. A flat white here tastes the same on a Tuesday as it does on a Saturday — that's the actual craft, not the latte art on top.

Roast Log

Beans go from the 12kg drum roaster to a finished bag in about 48 hours — roasted on site, rested overnight, then pulled the next morning. Nothing sits in a warehouse waiting on a reorder.

We sourced from 7 origins directly this year, and we run exactly one blend at a time. It changes when the harvest does — not when an invoice says it should.

Origins

The bag says where it's from. All of it.

Farm or cooperative, altitude, process, harvest date — on every retail bag, not just the ones that sound impressive. If we can't tell you where a bean came from, it doesn't go on the shelf.
