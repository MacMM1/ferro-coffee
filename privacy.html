Privacy & Cookies

What we do with your data.

Who we are

Ferro Coffee Co., Arch 12, Spa Road, Bermondsey, London SE16. Contact: hello@ferrocoffee.co.uk.

Cookies we use

This website uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use it — which pages get looked at, roughly how people found us, and general device/location information (like "mobile, UK"). It doesn't identify you personally, and we don't run any advertising or retargeting scripts.

You choose whether this runs. On your first visit, a banner asks you to accept or decline. If you decline, no analytics cookie is set and no data is sent to Google. You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of every page.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time — email hello@ferrocoffee.co.uk.
